1. What Marketplace HELP is

Marketplace HELP is a free extension for the Chrome web browser. It adds a few tools to Facebook Marketplace that make it easier to compare listings while you shop: marks for the listings you like (or don’t), a short list that stays at the front, saved searches, private notes, a large photo viewer, and a few helpful labels on listings you’ve opened.

Marketplace HELP is not affiliated with Meta or Facebook.

Everything it remembers stays in your own Chrome browser on this computer. Nothing is uploaded, there is no account to create, and nobody else can see your marks or notes.

2. Installing it

  1. Install Marketplace HELP from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open Facebook Marketplace in Chrome. If you already had a Marketplace page open, reload it.
  3. The Marketplace HELP buttons now appear right on the listings.
There is no toolbar button.

Don’t look for a Marketplace HELP icon next to Chrome’s address bar. All of the controls appear on the Facebook Marketplace page itself, on the listings, under the search box, and on an open listing.

3. The three marks on a card

Each listing in your search results gets a small row of three round buttons in the top-right corner of its photo. From left to right:

  • Trophy (🏆): finalist. Puts the listing on your short list. Finalists move to the front of the results (see the next section). The trophy lights up in gold when it’s selected.
  • Star (★): favorite. Marks a listing you like. The listing photo gets a gold glow around its inside edge so it stands out.
  • Slashed circle (⊘): undesired. Marks a listing you’re not interested in. The listing turns gray and faded, but it stays where it was and you can still click it to open it.

How the marks work

  • A listing can have only one mark at a time. Choosing a different mark replaces the old one.
  • To remove a mark, click the selected button again.
  • The same three buttons also appear next to My Notes when you open a listing, so you can mark it from there too.
  • Marks are remembered for that particular listing, so they’re still there after a new search, after you reload the page, and after you close and reopen Chrome.
  • If you have Marketplace open in more than one tab, a mark you make in one tab shows up in the others too.

4. Finalists move to the front

Every listing you mark with the trophy is moved to the beginning of the search results, in the order you picked them. Your first finalist comes first, your second comes next, and so on. That way your short list is the first thing you see.

One exception: sometimes Facebook controls exactly where a listing sits on the page, for example in a special row or a layout that isn’t a normal grid of results. In that case Marketplace HELP leaves the listing where it is rather than risk hiding it. It is still marked as a finalist.

5. Hide undesired

Under the Marketplace search box there’s a small panel with a switch labeled Hide undesired. It sits above your saved searches.

  • Off (gray): listings you marked with the slashed circle stay in the results, gray and faded.
  • On (blue): every listing you marked with the slashed circle is hidden from the results.

The switch stays on until you turn it off. It stays on after you reload the page, open a new search, or restart Chrome, and it applies to every open Marketplace tab.

Turning the switch on or off never changes your marks. Your slashed-circle listings are still marked, just out of sight, and they come back as soon as you switch it off.

6. Saving a search

  1. Type what you’re looking for in the Marketplace search box.
  2. Click the save icon (💾) at the right end of the search box. The phrase is saved.
  3. Your saved phrases appear under Saved searches, just below the Hide undesired switch.
  4. Click a saved phrase any time to run that search again without retyping it.

The save icon is dimmed when the search box is empty or when that phrase is already saved.

There isn’t a button to delete a single saved search yet. Saved searches are removed if you uninstall the extension.

7. Notes on a listing

When you open a listing, a My Notes bar appears just below the listing’s title and price. Type anything you want to remember, such as questions for the seller, measurements, or how it compares to another listing.

  • You don’t need a save button. Your note saves on its own a moment after you stop typing, and again when you click away. A small “Saved” message confirms it.
  • The note belongs to that one listing. The next time you open the listing, your note is there.
  • To remove a note, delete all of its text.

When a listing has a See more link on its description, Marketplace HELP clicks it for you so the full description is showing.

8. Zoom detail image

On the left end of the My Notes bar is a blue button, ← Zoom detail image. Click it to open the listing’s photos in a full-screen viewer so you can look closely at condition, labels, and wear. Clicking a photo on the listing opens the same viewer.

To do this Do this
Zoom in or out Roll the mouse wheel, press + or - on the keyboard, or click the + and − buttons at the top
Move around a zoomed photo Click and drag the photo
Zoom in quickly, or back out Double-click the photo
See how far you’re zoomed in Look at the button between − and +. It shows the zoom, such as 2:1.
Go back to normal size Click that zoom button (it returns to 1:1), or press 0
See the next or previous photo Press the ← and → arrow keys, or click the arrows on the left and right sides of the screen
Close the viewer Press Esc, or click the × in the top-right corner

Photos can be zoomed up to five times their normal size. The counter at the bottom shows which photo you’re on, such as 2 / 7.

9. The badges on a card

After you’ve opened a listing once in this browser, its card in the search results shows three small labels along the bottom of the photo:

  • Bottom left: number of photos, such as 3 pics.
  • Bottom center: how long it has been listed, such as 6 HRS.
  • Bottom right: how many times you’ve opened it, such as 1 view or 2 views. This counts your visits only, not other shoppers’.

Listings you haven’t opened yet don’t have these labels.

The listed time keeps counting up. If you come back to a saved search a few days later, a listing that said 2 DYS will say 5 DYS, so it’s easy to tell what’s new and what has been sitting for a while.

A listing you opened with an older version of Marketplace HELP may show a plus sign, such as 2+ WKS. The plus means “more than”: more than two weeks.

10. The seller caution panel

When you open a listing, a colored panel may appear at the top of the My Notes bar, above your note. It points out seller details that are worth a second look.

What can make it appear

  • The seller’s Facebook account is new. Facebook shows it joined this year, or six months ago or less.
  • No reply history is shown. The listing doesn’t show anything about how the seller responds to messages.
  • No rating is shown, or the rating is below 4 out of 5.
  • The seller has 5 or more other listings. This may be a dealer rather than a private seller.

Each reason that applies is listed in the panel.

What the colors mean

  • Check the seller (yellow): one of the reasons above applies.
  • Be careful (red): two or more reasons apply, or the seller has 5 or more other listings.
Please keep in mind
  • The panel reads only what Facebook already shows on the listing page.
  • It saves nothing about the seller: no name, no profile, no photo.
  • It is not advice and not a judgment about a person. It’s only a reminder to look more carefully. If no panel appears, that does not mean the seller is trustworthy.

The panel only appears when the listing shows when the seller joined Facebook. If that date isn’t visible, Marketplace HELP has nothing to go on, so some listings will never show a panel.

11. The loading bar

While a Marketplace page is loading, or while Marketplace adds more results as you scroll, a thin strip appears across the very top of the window. It has a blue progress bar and a short message such as “Marketplace HELP waiting for page to load…”. When everything is ready it says “Marketplace HELP ready” and disappears on its own.

12. Where your information is kept

Marketplace HELP keeps everything in Chrome’s own storage for extensions, on this computer, in the Chrome profile you’re using:

  • Your marks (finalist, favorite, undesired)
  • The order you picked your finalists
  • Your notes
  • Your saved searches
  • Whether Hide undesired is on or off
  • Photo counts, view counts, and listed times for listings you’ve opened

There is no account, no server, and no tracking or analytics. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

Removing a mark or clearing a note deletes it. Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored, including saved searches, counts, and listed times.

For the full details, read the Marketplace HELP privacy policy.

13. Troubleshooting

I don’t see any Marketplace HELP buttons

Reload the Marketplace page. The extension only works on Facebook Marketplace pages (addresses starting with www.facebook.com/marketplace), so it won’t appear elsewhere on Facebook. Also remember there is no toolbar button; the controls are on the listings themselves.

A card has no photo, view, or listed-time labels

You haven’t opened that listing yet in this browser. Open it once and the labels appear when you return to the results. Very small cards may not show the labels.

No caution panel appears on a listing

That listing doesn’t show when the seller joined Facebook, so there’s nothing to check. Remember that no panel doesn’t mean the seller is trustworthy.

A finalist didn’t move to the front

Facebook is controlling where that listing sits, so Marketplace HELP left it in place on purpose rather than risk hiding it. It is still marked as a finalist.

My marks are gone on a different computer

That’s by design. Marks, notes, and saved searches stay on the computer and Chrome profile where you made them. They are never uploaded, so they don’t follow you to another computer or another Chrome profile.
